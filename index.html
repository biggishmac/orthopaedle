Orthopaedle

Six clues. Six guesses. One orthopaedic diagnosis.

Ten cases

How to play

Each case opens with one clue. Name the diagnosis. Every wrong guess, or pass, breaks a bone and unlocks the next clue. You have six bones.

Each guess comes back with a tile:

  • CorrectYou named the diagnosis.
  • Right chapterWrong diagnosis, but it sits in the same chapter as the answer.
  • Wrong chapterThe answer is filed somewhere else.

The chapters are Shoulder, Elbow & forearm, Hand & wrist, Spine, Hip & pelvis, Knee, Thigh & leg, Foot & ankle, Tumour and Systemic. A diagnosis tied to one body region is filed under that region. Tumours and systemic conditions that can turn up anywhere have their own chapters.
